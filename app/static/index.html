The Ranking Trade-off

A brand-new post with 1 vote vs. a day-old post with 50 votes?

Reddit: New post wins
Freshness offset beats initial vote deficits. Order is stable over time.
Hacker News: Day-old post wins
Needs 2+ votes to score above zero. Score decays with passing time.

Decay Parameter

45,000s 12.5 hours

Each order of magnitude (10x) in votes buys exactly 12.5 hours of staying power. Doubling votes buys ~3.8 hours.
